Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority

We do not collect, store, or share any of your data — ever.

📱 Everything Stays on Your Device

All information you enter or generate within the app lives entirely on your phone. Nothing is transmitted to our servers or any third party. We have no servers that receive your data.

🚫 No Data Collection

We do not collect personal information, usage statistics, analytics, crash reports, or any other data about you or how you use the app. What you do in the app is your business alone.

🤝 No Data Sharing

Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to share. We do not sell, rent, trade, or disclose your information to advertisers, partners, or any other third parties — not now, not ever.

🔐 No Account Required

The app works without requiring you to create an account or provide any personal details. Your identity remains completely anonymous to us.

📡 Internet Use — When Needed, Never Tracked

Some apps may require an internet connection for specific features — for example, downloading an AI/LLM model or fetching your public IP address. When internet access is used, it is solely to deliver the feature you requested. We do not log, store, or share any data transmitted during these requests, and we have no servers that retain information about you.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please reach out via the app store listing.
